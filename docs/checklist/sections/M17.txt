M17 DETAILED REQUIREMENTS

Subscription billing and entitlements

P0 core  |  Dependencies: M01

Make MFH plan prices, usage limits and subscription changes transparent while enforcing real provider-backed entitlements and preserving existing customer agreements.

Inspect existing billing and separate it from client payments

[ ] M17-001  Inspect existing subscription provider products, checkout sessions, price IDs, customer records and entitlement checks before editing; record working evidence and preserve customer/subscription identifiers, treating existing subscription changes as unauthorized unless standing authorization covers them.

[ ] M17-002  Place subscription controls in Settings > Billing with Plan, Usage, Payment method and Billing history tabs; clearly identify charges for MicroFreelanceHub membership rather than the business's client invoice payments.

[ ] M17-003  Show Current plan, Billing interval, Subscription status, Next renewal date, Next expected charge and provider connection state; derive financial dates/amounts from the provider and label missing or stale synchronization with a corrective action.

[ ] M17-004  Show Free with Choose a plan when no paid subscription exists and No billing charges yet for confirmed empty history; distinguish a failed billing lookup from no subscription, and retain the last verified plan with its sync timestamp.

Plan pricing and entitlements

[ ] M17-005  Configure the published target plans Free, Starter at USD 31 per month and Professional at USD 54 per month only after matching actual provider prices; display billing frequency and applicable additional charges before checkout.

[ ] M17-006  Define Free allowance as the first three distinct published agreements per business; drafts and previews consume nothing, retries and versions of one agreement count once, and display Agreements used with links to the counted records.

[ ] M17-007  Give paid plans unlimited ordinary active projects as the recommended entitlement; remove an obsolete one-active-project restriction only after inspecting its enforcement, and keep provider-backed AI, storage and messaging allowances separately visible.

[ ] M17-008  Create one authoritative entitlement table containing Feature key, Enabled plans, Limit type, Limit amount and Effective plan version; populate it from approved offers and existing commitments rather than letting page labels independently determine access.

[ ] M17-009  Keep proposed annual prices USD 300 and USD 540 unavailable for purchase until confirmed and backed by real annual provider prices; hide or disable annual checkout controls with a clear unavailable explanation and never market them as active offers.
