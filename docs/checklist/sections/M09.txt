M09 DETAILED REQUIREMENTS

Contracts and signatures

P0 core  |  Dependencies: M02, M05, M07

Capture intentional signatures on exact document versions and retain trustworthy records of what each participant accepted.

Inspect signing before changing it

[ ] M09-001  Inspect current contract storage, template expansion, signing links, provider integrations and signature evidence; test a synthetic agreement and determine which component establishes signer identity and document integrity.

[ ] M09-002  Compare every requirement below with that working path; retain verified equivalents, extend missing controls only and leave signing disabled if immutable content or trustworthy acceptance recording is unavailable.

Contract draft fields and content

[ ] M09-003  Provide Documents then Contracts with New contract and From template actions; require Title of 1–150 characters, Client and Project, and default a new agreement to Draft.

[ ] M09-004  Provide business party and client party panels with required display or legal name and signatory email; include optional address and representative title without inventing legal entity details.

[ ] M09-005  Show the linked project, document currency where amounts appear and version number; changing draft parties must revalidate merge fields and intended signers before sharing.

[ ] M09-006  Provide contract sections Scope, Deliverables, Schedule, Fees, Payment terms and Additional terms; allow editing and reordering original business text without labeling a template as universally legally sufficient.

[ ] M09-007  Insert saved client, business and project values through the approved merge-field picker; block sharing when required fields remain unresolved and show the exact source field to correct.

[ ] M09-008  Offer Add attachment for PDF, JPEG, PNG or WebP within configured limits; list each included attachment and preserve its exact content alongside the contract version.

[ ] M09-009  Provide Preview document and Download draft controls with a visible Draft label; preview must not create a signature, send an invitation or mark the document as accepted.

Recipients and signature controls

[ ] M09-010  Provide a recipients table with Name, Email, Recipient type and Status; Recipient type options Signer and Copy recipient, default Signer, with at least one required signer.

[ ] M09-011  For each signer, require Name and valid Email, plus Signer capacity options Client, Business representative and Additional signer; show this label in the review screen without treating it as verified legal authority.
