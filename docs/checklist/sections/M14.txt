M14 DETAILED REQUIREMENTS

Payments refunds payouts and disputes

P0 core  |  Dependencies: M02, M11

Maintain an auditable payment ledger that reconciles collections, external payments, refunds, disputes and payouts without treating authorization, processing or transfer states as interchangeable.

Inspect money movement and establish the payment workspace

[ ] M14-001  Trace current provider account connections, checkout creation, webhook handling, ledger entries and invoice settlement before editing; reuse verified paths, document every failing or missing requirement, and keep business collection separate from MFH subscription billing.

[ ] M14-002  Add Payments tabs Transactions, Payouts, Disputes and Pay links with Connection settings accessible only to permitted administrators; show provider name, masked account, Sandbox or Live mode, last sync and truthful connection status.

[ ] M14-003  List transactions with Date, Client, Project, Invoice, Method, Gross amount, Currency, Refunded, Fee, Net and Status; offer search plus date, client, project, currency, method and payment-status filters without aggregating unlike currencies.

[ ] M14-004  Use payment statuses Pending, Succeeded, Failed, Cancelled, Partially refunded, Refunded and Disputed; retain raw provider status alongside the normalized value, and show authorization holds and payout statuses in separate records.

[ ] M14-005  Show No transactions yet, No payouts yet or No disputes for confirmed empty results with appropriate setup or record-payment actions; offer Clear filters for filtered emptiness and Retry for retrieval failures without fabricating zero balances.

Ledger and external payment recording

[ ] M14-006  Create immutable ledger events for successful collections, payment applications, credits, fees, refunds, chargebacks and reversals; attach provider IDs or external references, actor, currency and effective timestamp, and correct mistakes with linked reversals rather than deletion.

[ ] M14-007  Calculate amount due as issued total plus debit adjustments minus credit adjustments minus net applied receipts, floored at zero; exclude tips and processor fees, and display any excess as unapplied credit rather than negative invoice debt.

[ ] M14-008  Derive invoice payment progress from net applied receipts against the adjusted collectible principal; change lifecycle to Paid only after a supporting settlement event, label credited/refunded history explicitly, and never count waived charges as cash received.

[ ] M14-009  Provide Record external payment with required Client, Amount, Currency, Received date and Method choices Cash, Check, External bank transfer or Other; add optional Project, Invoice, Reference, Private note and receipt attachment fields.
