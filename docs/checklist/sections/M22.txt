M22 DETAILED REQUIREMENTS

Recurring service appointments

P1 expansion  |  Dependencies: M19, M20, M02, M05

Schedule repeat service visits with controlled exceptions and assignment history while keeping visit recurrence separate from billing recurrence.

Inspect and locate recurring visits

[ ] M22-001  Inspect existing recurring availability, appointment series and project-task implementations separately from M13 recurring invoices. Test current recurrence behavior first; reuse verified models and clearly label service visit recurrence as scheduling rather than automatic billing.

[ ] M22-002  Provide Calendar > Service series and a related Project > Visits tab. List Client, Service, Assigned staff, Repeat rule, Timezone, Next visit and Series state; filter by Active, Paused or Ended without deleting historical visits.

[ ] M22-003  Provide New service series, View visits, Edit series, Pause, Resume and End series actions. Require assignment-scoped permission to modify a series and preserve equivalent working controls rather than adding a second scheduling system.

[ ] M22-004  Require a saved Client, a Service or service title, and a Start date/time. Allow an optional existing Project and service address; validate the project belongs to the selected client and tenant before saving.

Series fields and generation rules

[ ] M22-005  Require an IANA Series timezone and preserve the intended local service time across daylight-saving changes. Default to the business/service-location timezone already saved; show a preview of the next five local dates and UTC offsets.

[ ] M22-006  Offer Visit duration values 15, 30, 45, 60, 90 and 120 minutes plus Custom from 5 to 720 minutes. Show travel/setup buffers separately and use M20 conflict rules without charging for buffer time implicitly.

[ ] M22-007  Offer Repeat values Daily, Weekly, Every two weeks, Monthly and Custom. For Custom require an interval of 1–52 days/weeks or 1–12 months; display a plain-language summary before the user saves the series.

[ ] M22-008  For weekly recurrence provide weekday checkboxes Monday through Sunday and require at least one. Anchor alternating weeks to the stated series start week and never generate an occurrence before the selected start date.

Series fields and generation rules

[ ] M22-009  For monthly recurrence offer On day with choices 1–31 or On weekday with First, Second, Third, Fourth or Last plus a weekday. Require one mode and preserve the rule rather than drifting from a shortened month.

[ ] M22-010  For a monthly day that is absent, offer Skip that month or Use the last valid day; default Skip that month for new service series. Show February/short-month examples in the recurrence preview and store the selected rule explicitly.

[ ] M22-011  Offer End rule values No end date, On date or After number of visits. Require an inclusive local end date or a whole count from 1 to 1,000; count intended occurrences consistently and explain whether skipped exceptions consume an occurrence.

[ ] M22-012  Define an occurrence count as the number of scheduled rule instances before manual exceptions: a skipped visit retains its occurrence identity and count. Restoring a skipped occurrence must not extend a count-limited series or create an extra visit.

[ ] M22-013  For daylight-saving gaps offer Move forward by the gap or Skip occurrence, default Move forward by the gap. Use the earlier offset for repeated fall-back times and expose the resolved offset in the preview and occurrence details.

[ ] M22-014  Generate a bounded rolling window, proposed 90 days, rather than infinitely materializing an unending series. Show the generated-through date; extend the window through the existing verified worker or a clearly labeled Generate upcoming visits action.

[ ] M22-015  Assign a stable identity to each intended local occurrence and generate idempotently. Repeating or retrying generation must not duplicate a visit, assigned task, reminder or linked invoice candidate.

[ ] M22-016  Provide Assigned staff and optional Required equipment selectors using verified M24 permissions and capacity. Let an authorized manager specify a series default; an exception may change one visit without rewriting earlier assignments.

Visit records and series changes

[ ] M22-017  Use visit states Scheduled, In progress, Completed, Skipped, Cancelled and No show, separate from series state and payment state. Show actual start/end only when recorded; marking Completed must not create a charge or fake a payment.

Visit records and series changes

[ ] M22-018  Provide visit fields Client instructions, Internal notes and optional task checklist linked to M05. Keep client-visible instructions separate from staff-only details and preserve the version of instructions used for a completed visit.

[ ] M22-022  Implement This and following visits by creating an effective-dated recurrence change. Preserve the old rule and historical occurrences; re-evaluate future slot conflicts and show any visits needing manual resolution before committing.

[ ] M22-027  Mark a conflicting future occurrence Needs scheduling in a separate scheduling-condition field without falsely confirming it. Offer Change time, Reassign staff or Skip visit; do not silently drop the occurrence or override another reservation.

Observable recurring visit acceptance checks

[ ] M22-031  Create a weekly synthetic 09:00 America/New_York series crossing a daylight-saving transition. Confirm each valid visit remains at 09:00 local time, offsets change correctly and the chosen gap/fold rules are reproducible.

[ ] M22-032  Create a monthly series for day 31 and inspect February, April and the following month. Confirm Skip and Last valid day behave as configured and the original anchor does not drift permanently to an earlier day.

[ ] M22-033  Run Generate upcoming visits twice and retry one uncertain result. Confirm the same occurrence identifiers and visit count remain, with no duplicate tasks, reminder jobs or billing actions.

[ ] M22-034  Move one visit, skip another and change all following visits. Confirm generation respects those exceptions, completed visits remain unchanged and the client/project sees the correct upcoming schedule.

[ ] M22-035  Pause and resume a series containing a paid future visit. Confirm the appointment changes follow the selected preview, the payment ledger remains unchanged and no refund or recurring charge occurs automatically.

[ ] M22-036  Open a series as assigned staff, a bookkeeper and an unrelated business user. Confirm staff see permitted work, bookkeepers only authorized financial links, and the unrelated business cannot enumerate or modify series occurrences.
