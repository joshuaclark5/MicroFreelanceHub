M13 DETAILED REQUIREMENTS

Recurring billing

P1 expansion  |  Dependencies: M11, M12, M14

Operate transparent recurring invoice schedules and authorized recurring collection with one traceable invoice per billing occurrence and client-visible future obligations.

Inspect existing recurrence and expose the plan list

[ ] M13-001  Inspect existing weekly/monthly recurring templates, generation buttons and background jobs; verify each behavior before editing, preserve working templates, and label manual draft generation honestly until scheduled generation and collection are independently verified.

[ ] M13-002  Add Invoices > Recurring plans with Create recurring plan and columns Plan, Client, Project, Currency, Amount, Cadence, Next invoice, Next collection and Status; provide All, Draft, Active, Paused, Completed and Cancelled filters.

[ ] M13-003  Give plan detail Overview, Future schedule, Invoices, Payment method and Activity tabs; show any Attention required badge separately from lifecycle, and explain missing provider permission, consent or payment method beside a corrective action.

[ ] M13-004  Show No recurring plans yet with Create recurring plan and No matching plans with Clear filters; show No invoices generated on a new plan and preserve the schedule preview when invoice-history loading fails.

Plan fields and recurrence rules

[ ] M13-005  Require Plan name, Client, Currency, First invoice date and business IANA Timezone; make Project optional, default timezone from business settings, and prevent mixed-currency items or changes to currency after the first published occurrence.

[ ] M13-006  Provide Cadence choices Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Monthly, Every 3 months and Yearly; default Monthly, persist the original anchor, and display a readable sentence describing the selected schedule before activation.

[ ] M13-007  For monthly schedules, offer Billing day choices 1 through 31 and Last day; clamp unavailable days while retaining the anchor, so January 31 returns to March 31, and apply equivalent clamping to quarterly/yearly anchors including February 29.

[ ] M13-008  Require local Generation time, default 09:00; calculate occurrences in the saved timezone before storing UTC, use the earlier offset for repeated clock times and the first valid instant after a nonexistent local time, and show that adjustment.
