Reports exports and forecasts

P1 expansion  |  Dependencies: M11, M14, M15

Provide explainable reports and exportable source records while separating invoice face value, actual collections, outstanding obligations and forecast assumptions.

Inspect existing reporting definitions

[ ] M16-001  Inspect every existing report, aggregation and CSV export against source records before editing; retain verified behavior and explicitly relabel invoice face value or recorded activity that is currently presented as cash received, profit or forecast.

[ ] M16-002  Provide Reports choices Overview, Collections, Receivables, Expenses, Project profit, Time, Client value, Team and Cash forecast; show metric definitions, No matching records with Clear filters, and distinct Loading or Failed with Retry states rather than fabricated zero totals.

[ ] M16-003  Place Date range, Client, Project and Currency filters above results with This month, Last month, This quarter, This year and Custom choices; add As-of date, default today, for balance/aging snapshots and preserve authorized URL filters and business timezone.

Financial report definitions and drill downs

[ ] M16-004  Label each report's Date basis explicitly: Invoice issued date, Invoice due date, Payment received date, Expense incurred date or Time entry date as applicable; default by report purpose and never switch bases without refreshing the explanatory label.

[ ] M16-005  Show Overview metrics Gross invoiced, Invoice credits, Net collected, Outstanding and Overdue from authoritative records; exclude Draft/Void principal, label period flows versus As-of balances, reconstruct snapshots using effective ledger timestamps, and link each metric to contributing rows.

[ ] M16-006  Define Net collected as succeeded gross receipts less successful refunds, excluding pending/failed payments; show processor fees, net settlement and tips separately, and prevent payouts or recorded invoice totals from becoming a second copy of collection revenue.

[ ] M16-007  Define Outstanding from M14's adjusted unpaid principal across eligible invoices at the selected As-of date; derive Overdue when the due date precedes that local date, show Due today separately, and exclude later ledger events from historical snapshots.

[ ] M16-008  Provide invoice status choices All, Draft, Open, Partially paid, Paid, Overdue, Void and Uncollectible; define Partially paid as positive applied receipts below collectible principal and Overdue as the due predicate, while retaining the underlying lifecycle.
