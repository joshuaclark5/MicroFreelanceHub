M18 DETAILED REQUIREMENTS

Financial partner services

P2 provider dependent  |  Dependencies: M14, M17

Expose optional banking, financing and payment services only through verified providers, with truthful eligibility, lifecycle controls and explicit blockers instead of simulated financial products.

Inspect providers and make readiness explicit

[ ] M18-001  Inspect actual provider contracts, enabled capabilities, credentials, supported countries/currencies and existing integrations for every service before editing; preserve verified implementations, record exact unmet dependencies, and leave unsupported services unchecked rather than promising completion within 48 hours.

[ ] M18-002  Place optional service cards under Settings > Integrations > Financial services and contextual Payments/Project links; show provider, Sandbox/Live and Disabled, Not connected, Connecting, Connected or Attention required, with No eligible service available for confirmed empty eligibility and Retry for lookup failure.

[ ] M18-003  Require Owner-controlled connection/consent actions and show eligibility separately from connection; display provider-sourced restrictions, current terms/fee links and last verification time, and never infer approval or availability from a purchased MFH subscription.

Bank accounts savings and tax categories

[ ] M18-004  For a supported banking service, provide Connect account, Open provider portal, Refresh status and Disconnect; collect identity/country/ownership through its approved flow, show required verification, and explain that disconnecting MFH synchronization does not close the bank account.

[ ] M18-005  List connected accounts with provider, masked account identifier, Currency, Available balance, Current balance, Pending amount and Updated at; show Unavailable or Stale when retrieval fails instead of substituting local invoice balances as bank balances.

[ ] M18-006  Expose Transfer or Funding controls only when the provider supports the operation and the account is eligible; require source, destination, amount/currency and a current fee/arrival preview, and use explicit confirmation before any real money movement.

[ ] M18-007  Show transfer states Draft, Submitted, Processing, Succeeded, Failed or Cancelled from provider evidence; deduplicate requests, explain unavailable cancellation, and prevent a failed/unknown transfer from appearing as settled cash in M16.

[ ] M18-008  For savings allocations, provide Bucket name, Currency, Target amount and Allocation rule Manual, Fixed amount or Percentage; distinguish a real provider bucket/transfer from an internal budgeting label and prevent allocations exceeding available authorized funds.

Security card authorization holds

[ ] M18-020  Place Security hold under Project > Event details or Payments when actual merchant/provider eligibility permits; show maximum amount, allowed duration, supported methods and capture/release rules from current capability data, separately from ordinary invoice deposits.

[ ] M18-021  Provide Create hold with required Client, Project, Amount, Currency, Authorization date and Intended release date plus optional Description; require provider-supported client authorization/consent, validate duration/amount limits, and do not record the held amount as income.

[ ] M18-022  Show hold states Draft, Awaiting authorization, Authorized, Partially captured, Captured, Released, Expired and Failed, with remaining authorized amount and provider expiry; show real provider limitations when partial capture or extended holds are unsupported.

[ ] M18-023  Provide Capture all or Capture amount only within the remaining valid authorization; require amount and reason, preview the resulting charge, create a separate M14 payment after confirmed capture, and prohibit capture after release/expiry without fresh authorization.

[ ] M18-024  Provide Release hold with a confirmation showing the amount to release; display Release requested until provider confirmation, explain any provider-reported bank release delay, and never describe releasing an uncharged hold as refunding a collected payment.

[ ] M18-025  Default hold creation/capture/release to Owner/Admin with explicit grants for other financial roles; validate permissions server-side, deduplicate hold events, log every action and never mark an invoice paid merely because authorization succeeded.
