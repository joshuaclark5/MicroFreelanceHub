M12 DETAILED REQUIREMENTS

Invoice collection and pay links

P0 core  |  Dependencies: M11, M14

Turn verified invoices into clear payment requests, schedules and client-specific checkout links without confusing drafts, authorizations or pending funds with collected money.

Inspect collection and expose honest readiness

[ ] M12-001  Trace the existing invoice, agreement and Stripe Connect collection paths before editing; test each requirement with synthetic data and actual provider test mode where available, preserve working controls, and record unavailable credentials as blockers.

[ ] M12-002  Add Collection and Payment schedule tabs to invoice detail; show provider readiness as Disabled, Not connected, Connecting, Connected or Attention required, with Set up payments or Review connection actions for permitted business administrators.

[ ] M12-003  Show invoice lifecycle, payment progress and due condition separately above Total, Paid, Credits and Amount due; make Pay invoice unavailable for Draft, Void or Uncollectible records and explain the reason beside the control.

Payment schedule and deposits

[ ] M12-004  Provide Payment schedule choices Full amount, Deposit and balance, Equal installments and Custom installments; default to Full amount, and link Recurring billing to M13 without turning an installment schedule into a subscription.

[ ] M12-005  For Deposit and balance, require Deposit type Fixed amount or Percentage and a positive value; default the remaining balance to the invoice due date, and block deposits exceeding the collectible invoice principal.

[ ] M12-006  For Equal installments, require Count between 2 and 24, First due date and Interval Weekly, Every 2 weeks or Monthly; clamp unavailable monthly days while retaining the original anchor and preview every date/amount before Save schedule.

[ ] M12-007  For each custom installment, require Label, Amount type Fixed or Percentage and Due rule On receipt, Specific date, Days after issue, Days before project date or Days after project date; request required date/offset inputs conditionally.

[ ] M12-008  Reject missing project dates, duplicate installment identifiers, nonpositive amounts and schedules whose amounts do not equal the invoice balance; use currency minor units and assign rounding remainder to the final unpaid installment.

[ ] M12-009  Provide Edit schedule only for unpaid portions; preserve paid installments and historical versions, show before/after dates and amounts, and require a reason before applying changes that affect an already shared invoice.

Send and share payment requests

[ ] M12-015  List pay links with Client, Purpose, Amount, Currency, Created, Expires, Status and Paid amount; offer All, Active, Paid, Expired and Revoked filters, treating partial links as Active, with Copy link, View checkout, Revoke and Create replacement actions.

[ ] M12-016  Bind each link to its intended client and payable purpose; reject tampered amounts, expired/revoked links and double submission, use idempotent checkout creation, and distinguish unallocated client payments from payments applied to an invoice.

[ ] M12-017  Show branded checkout with business identity, client-visible invoice or description, currency, amount, due date and supported methods; include View invoice and payment-support contact controls without exposing other clients or internal costs.

Client payment options
