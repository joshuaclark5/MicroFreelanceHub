M24 DETAILED REQUIREMENTS

Team roles assignments and resource capacity

P1 expansion  |  Dependencies: M02, M05

Give each teammate the correct access and make staff and equipment capacity visible without exposing private business records.

Inspect and organize team management

[ ] M24-001  Inspect existing solo records, separate team-planning workspaces, invitations, membership checks and allocation tables. Prove each requested behavior before editing; preserve verified private boundaries and never copy all solo clients or finances into a shared team automatically.

[ ] M24-002  Place Members, Invitations and Roles under Settings > Team. Place Workload and Equipment under the existing Projects or Calendar resource view with a link from Team; avoid a separate disconnected roster.

[ ] M24-003  List Member name, Verified email, Role, Access scope, Invitation state and Last active where supported. Filter Active, Invited and Deactivated; provide View access, Change role and Remove access only to permitted administrators.

[ ] M24-004  Provide Invite member with Email required, Role default Member and optional assigned projects. Offer Save invitation, Copy invitation link and Send invitation through a verified provider; saving an invitation must not claim an email was delivered.

[ ] M24-005  Show the actual seat allowance, current active seats and any price impact before adding paid seats. Do not charge or change a subscription from a role editor; route authorized plan changes through M17.

Roles invitation lifecycle and record sharing

[ ] M24-006  Enforce Owner as the role that controls ownership and subscription changes. Provide ownership transfer only through its existing explicit verified workflow; an Admin or modified client request must not promote itself to Owner.

[ ] M24-007  Enforce Admin access to authorized business operations and team management while withholding ownership transfer and subscription mutation. Display Billing read-only only with an authorized billing-view grant, and route subscription changes to the Owner under M17.

[ ] M24-008  Enforce Manager access to assigned projects/clients and document drafts. Keep global billing/security and refund actions unavailable by default; assigning one project must not reveal all customer projects or private financial records.

[ ] M24-009  Enforce Member access to assigned work and their permitted time records. Require an explicit financial/internal permission before exposing prices, invoices, internal notes or communications beyond what is needed for that assignment.

[ ] M24-010  Enforce Bookkeeper access to authorized invoices, payments, expenses and reports without private conversations. Do not grant document-signature authority, client messaging or private calendar access merely because the role can see financial totals.

[ ] M24-011  Keep Client as an external portal participant, never a team role granting internal access. A client invitation exposes only selected shared project data and cannot be used to accept an internal team invitation.

People and equipment capacity

[ ] M24-017  Provide each active worker's capacity settings with Timezone, Working weekdays and Hours per weekday. Leave new capacity Unconfigured until reviewed; do not silently assume that every invitee is available forty hours each week.

[ ] M24-018  Accept daily capacity from 0 to 24 hours with an explicit effective date and week-start preference. Distinguish capacity hours from the appointment windows in M20; changing weekly capacity alone must not move client appointments.

[ ] M24-019  Calculate available capacity as scheduled capacity minus applicable time-off hours, floored at zero. Show original capacity, time off and remaining available hours separately, using the worker's timezone for day/week boundaries.

[ ] M24-020  Calculate planned load from linked task allocations and service appointments without counting the same linked visit twice. Show the contributing records and distinguish planned effort from billable hours or recorded actual time.

[ ] M24-021  Provide Day and Week workload views with Member, Available hours, Planned hours, Remaining hours and Utilization columns. Compute utilization only when available hours exceed zero; show No capacity rather than dividing by zero.

[ ] M24-022  Provide Assign work with an existing Project, optional Task or Visit, Staff member, Date range and Estimated hours. Require positive estimates in quarter-hour increments and save one linked allocation rather than an unrelated billing record.
