## App name

MicroFreelanceHub

## Short description

View your existing projects and record completed, nonbillable work time.

## Full description

Keep a focused view of your MicroFreelanceHub projects and recent work time.

Sign in to your MicroFreelanceHub account with Google or your email password. View up to 200 active projects, search the loaded project list, and see your latest 20 completed time entries.

Record completed work against an existing project. Enter the duration and an optional note, review the details, then confirm the save. Entries recorded here are nonbillable: saving time does not create an invoice or charge a customer.

If a save cannot be confirmed, keep the app open and retry the same entry. Unsaved drafts and pending retry details are not stored for offline recovery and can be lost when the app closes. An internet connection is required to load or save work.

This companion does not create projects or provide calendar, booking, agreement, invoice or payment screens. You need an active project already in your account to record time. A new account without projects will show an empty project list.

Privacy information and an account-deletion request action are available in the app. The request action opens an email draft; it does not send automatically or complete deletion.
