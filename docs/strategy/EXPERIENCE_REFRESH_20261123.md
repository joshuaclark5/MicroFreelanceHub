# Experience refresh: homepage, onboarding and workspace Home

## Scope and references
Joshua supplied thirteen HoneyBook screenshots and explicitly requested implementation across the homepage, dashboard and onboarding. Prior website publication authorization remains in scope. Inspiration: task-led entry, restrained typography, clear application navigation, short setup and visible progress. This is an original MicroFreelanceHub design, not a copy of competitor assets, claims or offers.

## Acceptance by surface
- Homepage: product-category heading, primary workspace action, fictional labeled product preview, six working goal selectors. Existing substantive lower-page content, profession/template/article links, pricing and sample agreement retained.
- Login: existing Google/email authentication, password recovery, plan/template attribution priority; new side-by-side workspace preview on desktop. No invented trial or entitlement change.
- Setup: two steps, business name, optional profession, first goal, back/skip/help, owner-bound preference save, failure/retry, saved confirmation and direct tool destination. No customer records, emails, subscriptions or charges created by setup.
- Home: authenticated customer/project/task data, completeness checking, read-only search, next-task links, real-record checklist, empty/loading/error states and owner continuity checks. No fabricated revenue or billing totals.
- Navigation: consistent forest-green rail, icons and Tasks entry, mobile menu, always-visible account actions.

## Deliberate differences from supplied references
No competitor branding, stock customer revenue, fake discounts, AI-setup promises, integration badges or unverified trial. Existing agreement dashboard remains at /dashboard; the all-tools Home is /workspace. This release does not redesign every individual editor or change mobile native binaries.
